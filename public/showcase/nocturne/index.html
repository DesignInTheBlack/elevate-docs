The wheels change while you sleep

Portugal and Spain lay their rails wider apart than France does. At Hendaye the train rolls through a gauge changer at walking pace, and every wheel slides to the new width.

Questions

Before you board

Can I ride only part of the way?
Yes. Every stop sells tickets to every later stop, and a berth from Salamanca to Bordeaux costs less than the full run.
Will anyone wake me at the borders?
No. Portugal, Spain and France are all in the Schengen area, so there are no passport checks on board. Keep your ID with you anyway.
Does the gauge change mean changing trains?
No. The train's axles are built to slide between the two gauges, so you stay in your cabin while it rolls through.
Can I bring my dog?
Small dogs travel free in a Sleeper or Suite you've booked whole. Couchettes are shared, so pets stay home on those.
Where do I find the train in Paris?
Paris-Austerlitz, beside the Jardin des Plantes. The N2 boards forty minutes before it leaves.

---
title: "Object fit"
description: "Decide how images and video fill their box."
---

Images and video keep their own proportions by default and can stretch or overflow their box. `fit` decides how they fill it.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fit` | `fill`, `contain`, `cover`, `none`, `scale-down` | `object-fit` | `fit:cover` |
| `object-position` | A pass-through value | `object-position` | `object-position:(center_top)` |

`cover` fills the box and crops the overflow. `contain` shows the whole image and leaves space around it. Use `object-position` to choose which part stays in view, with `_` in place of spaces.

```html title="A square avatar that keeps faces in frame"
<img class="aspect:square w:d12 fit:cover object-position:(center_top) bd:round-d8" src="…" alt="">
```

---
title: "Scroll spacing"
description: "Leave room when the page scrolls to an anchor or snap point."
---

With a sticky header, jumping to `#pricing` hides the heading under the header. `scroll-margin` on the target, or `scroll-padding` on the scroller, leaves room for it.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scroll-margin` | One [spacing token](/default/spacing/) | `scroll-margin` | `scroll-margin:c1` |
| `scroll-padding` | One spacing token | `scroll-padding` | `scroll-padding:d4` |

Both currently take a single value that applies to all sides.

```html title="Headings that land below a 4rem sticky header"
<h2 id="pricing" class="scroll-margin:c1">Pricing</h2>
```

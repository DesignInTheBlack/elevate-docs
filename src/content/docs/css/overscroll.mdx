---
title: "Overscroll"
description: "Control what happens when a scroll area hits its edge."
---

By default, scrolling past the end of a panel carries on into the page behind it. `overscroll:contain` keeps the scroll inside the panel.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `overscroll` | `auto`, `contain`, `none` | `overscroll-behavior` | `overscroll:contain` |
| `overscroll-x` | `auto`, `contain`, `none` | `overscroll-behavior-x` | `overscroll-x:none` |
| `overscroll-y` | `auto`, `contain`, `none` | `overscroll-behavior-y` | `overscroll-y:contain` |

```html title="A chat panel that doesn't scroll the page behind it"
<div class="overflow-y:auto overscroll-y:contain">…</div>
```

---
title: "Resize"
description: "Let people drag to resize an element."
---

Text areas are resizable in both directions by default. Limit it to one direction, or turn it off.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `resize` | `none`, `both`, `block`, `inline`, `horizontal`, `vertical` | `resize` | `resize:vertical` |

```html
<textarea class="resize:vertical w:full"></textarea>
```

---
title: "Scroll behavior"
description: "Animate jumps to anchors and programmatic scrolling."
---

Put `scroll-behavior:smooth` on the scrolling element, often `<html>`, and in-page links glide instead of jumping.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scroll-behavior` | `auto`, `smooth` | `scroll-behavior` | `scroll-behavior:smooth` |

```html
<html class="scroll-behavior:smooth">
```

---
title: "Accent color"
description: "Tint checkboxes, radio buttons, range sliders and progress bars with a color token."
---

Browsers draw form controls in their own colors. `accent` swaps the highlight for any color from your palette.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accent` | Any [color token](/default/colors/) | `accent-color` | `accent:indigo` |

```html title="Checkboxes and sliders in your brand color"
<input type="checkbox" class="accent:indigo">
<input type="range" class="accent:pink-1 w:full">
```

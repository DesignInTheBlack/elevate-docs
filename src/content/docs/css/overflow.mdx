---
title: Overflow
description: "Clip, scroll or show content that doesn't fit."
---

Decide what happens to content that doesn't fit its box.

| Property | Values | Compiles to | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `overflow` | `visible`, `hidden`, `scroll`, `auto` | `overflow` | `overflow:hidden` |
| `overflow-x` | Same | `overflow-x` | `overflow-x:auto` |
| `overflow-y` | Same | `overflow-y` | `overflow-y:auto` |

`auto` shows a scrollbar only when it's needed. `scroll` always shows one. `hidden` clips the content.

```html title="A wide table that scrolls sideways on small screens"
<div class="overflow-x:auto">
  <table>…</table>
</div>
```
